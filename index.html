    Tonight’s seven

      How to play

      1. Each night has seven categories, the same for everyone. You get 30 seconds for each.
      2. Type anything that fits. If it counts, every letter you haven’t caught yet flies into your jar as a firefly.
      3. Rare letters glow brighter. An E is worth 4 lumens; a Q or Z is worth 50.
      4. Catch all 26 to seal the jar for +100 lumens. A fresh jar opens for the rest of the night.
      5. All seven categories are listed from the start, so plan where your Q, Z, J and X will come from.
      6. Wrong answers cost nothing. Keep trying until the wick burns out, or pass.

      Playing with friends

      Press Play with friends to open a party. Friends who have this page open see it under Here now and can join with one tap, the four-letter code or your invite link. Everyone gets the same category at the same moment. When two players give the same answer, both get half the lumens. Finish the nightly hunt and your score lands on Tonight’s board.

      Answer lists are hand-built, so the odd valid answer may be missing. Small typos are forgiven.
